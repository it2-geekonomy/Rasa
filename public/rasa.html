FAQ and policies

Why RASA

Bhaava is the artist's feeling. Rasa is what it becomes in you.

Bhaava is the emotional state or expression portrayed by the artist through their performance.

Rasa is the aesthetic experience or essence savoured by the audience as a result of that artistic expression.

A charitable cultural festival bringing together global and local artists to create meaningful collaborations rooted in India's rich artistic heritage. RASA is an immersive experience that celebrates our traditions while connecting the mind, body and soul.

Four strands

Music

Concerts, instrumentalists and contemporary collaborations.

Dance

Classical and contemporary movement, for enthusiasts and new audiences alike.

Wellness

Sound healing, yoga and immersive experiences that sit alongside the arts.

Culture

Art workshops, fusion food and community.

What sets it apart

Curated

A journey across music, dance, wellness and culture.

Immersive

You can watch, learn, take part and explore within one festival.

Across generations

Classical traditions and contemporary collaborations appeal to a wide audience, with time alongside artists from around the world.

Celebrate Art. Celebrate Culture. Celebrate Togetherness. Making a difference.

About Anjali

Anjali Ajaikumar is a healthcare leader, strategist and social entrepreneur. She is Director at Healthcare Global Enterprises Ltd. (HCG) and at Milann Fertility & Birthing Hospital, a Board Member and Trustee at Bharath Hospital of Oncology (BHIO), Mysuru, and Director and Trustee at HCG Foundation.

With more than 17 years in healthcare administration, strategic leadership and organisational development, she has built centralised functions in quality assurance, clinical excellence, operations and nursing across all 25 HCG centres in India and overseas. She holds an MBA in Entrepreneurship from Babson College, serves on the Board of Advisors of the KMH Center for Health Innovation and Entrepreneurship at Babson, and is Co-founder of Lumos Health Advisory.

She champions cancer awareness, has led fundraising for patients facing financial hardship, and is a Trustee of the International Human Development and Upliftment Academy (IHDUA), which supports rural education, women's empowerment and environmental sustainability.

About HCG Foundation

Ensure access to quality cancer treatment for those in need. Create greater awareness about cancer and its prevention.

At RASA, the foundation shows curated art displays on Saturday and Sunday, about art for healing, community and social impact, and Aroghya hosts the organic food breakfast on Sunday. Aroghya restaurant is part of the HCG group of hospitals.

Women Empowerment and education

Skill training, self-help groups, model villages & education around Mysore and Gundlupet districts.

About IHDUA

International Human Development and Upliftment Academy (IHDUA)

A non-profit based in Mysore and Gundlupet, established in 1991 and founded by Dr. B S Ajaikumar. It works on rural education, women's empowerment, skill development and model villages, with around 500 self-help groups today.
